⚡ AI Generation & Workout Customization

How does FitBuddy create my personalized workout split?

FitBuddy takes your age, current weight, primary fitness goal (e.g. Muscle Gain, Fat Loss, Flexibility, General Fitness), and desired intensity level, and feeds this into Google Gemini AI with sports-science prompting templates. It outputs a full 7-day periodized split with precise rep schemes, warm-ups, rest periods, and cooldowns.

Can I adjust or revise my workout plan after it is generated?

Yes! Every plan has a dedicated "Adjust Plan" / "✏️ Revise" feature. You can request specific changes—such as replacing barbell exercises with dumbbells, adding more core work, or including an extra recovery day—and Gemini AI will recalculate and update the entire plan while preserving your history.

What is the difference between Low, Medium, and High intensity?

Low intensity focuses on foundational movement patterns, active recovery, and low joint impact (great for beginners or deload weeks). Medium intensity balances hypertrophy, cardio conditioning, and manageable volume. High intensity incorporates advanced progressive overload, supersets, high-density intervals, and demanding training volume.

🔥 Progress Streaks & Community

How do workout streaks and daily chips work?

In your Athlete Dashboard, every saved 7-day program features interactive Day 1 through Day 7 chips (D1, D2, ..., D7). Clicking a day marks that session completed in our database, which updates your current streak, longest streak, and completion rate in real-time.

How do I share my workout to the global community feed?

In your Dashboard plan table, click the 🔒 Private / 🌐 Public toggle button on any saved plan. When toggled to Public, other athletes can discover, like, and draw inspiration from your routine on the Community Feed.
