Frequently Asked Billing Questions

How does billing work?

FitBuddy Pro subscriptions are handled securely by Stripe. You are billed monthly at $9.99 with no long-term contracts. You can cancel at any time directly through your dashboard.

Can I try FitBuddy for free?

Yes! Our Free Athlete tier provides 3 full AI plan generations every month with complete access to our community, streak tracking, and nutrition guidance without entering any payment information.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

If you cancel, you will maintain Pro access until the end of your current billing cycle. Afterward, your account gracefully transitions back to the Free Athlete tier while retaining all your past saved workouts.
