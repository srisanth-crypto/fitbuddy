Our Vision & Technology

Our Core Mission

Traditional personal training is often cost-prohibitive, while generic workout templates ignore individual biomechanics, goals, and recovery limits. FitBuddy leverages state-of-the-art LLMs to create bespoke fitness roadmaps adapted in real-time.

🔬

Scientific Methodology

Every workout routine generated by FitBuddy adheres to exercise physiology tenets: progressive overload, compound movement prioritization, adequate recovery windows, and tailored volume based on athlete training intensity.

🔒

Privacy & Athlete Safety

We believe your physiological data belongs to you. Passwords are encrypted with bcrypt, payments are delegated to PCI-compliant Stripe infrastructure, and your metric data is never sold or used for ad targeting.
